About Our App

Our app is an all in one app that allows students to have access to a tasklist, timer, study stop locator, and much more. This app is designed to help you increase your productivity and improve the effectiveness of your study sessions.

Tasklist

The tasklist feature an easy to use task management system, allowing you to prioritize tasks and sort them based on topic and improtance. In addition, the app will push notifications allowing you to be reminded of upcoming tasks that are due.

Calendar

The calendar feature allows you to view upcoming tasks. Allowing you to work ahead or to plan out your week in order to maximize your productivity.

Timer

The timer features a minimalistic timer, allowing you to concentrate on the task at hand. This timer also has the function to use popular study techniques like the Pomodoro Technique allowing for even more productivity and focus during study sessions. You can even use this to time your self during test prep.

Study Spot Locator

Our favorite feature, the study spot locator, allows you to easily find study spots near you. Whether you need wifi, a quiet place, or charging for your studies, we've got you covered with a variety of study spots that cater to anyone's needs.
